Chisme

Show news & weather for where you are?

Chisme uses your location for your forecast, alerts, radar, nearby stories, sports and Tía. It's saved on this device and sent to Chisme's server, rounded to about 1 km, to look things up; it's never sold. With notifications on, your area (about 1 km) stays on the server until you turn them off. You'll only be asked once: to change it later, tap the Chisme bubble at the top for Settings.

        Sports sources

        Scores, schedules, standings and news come from ESPN's public site API and the MLB Stats API. Your teams follow your location: the nearest NBA, NFL and MLB clubs, every team in your state first on the scoreboards, and the closest Minor League club within about 60 km (in San Antonio: the Spurs and the Double-A Missions). Story photos are the thumbnails ESPN publishes with each story and link to it; team photos are freely licensed Wikimedia Commons images, credited on each photo. No team logos. Headlines and stories belong to their publishers.

                    Notifications

                    Only when it really matters: big local or breaking news and NWS warnings near you. Nothing is sent until you turn it on.

                    News notifications: at most 2 a day, only for major San Antonio-area or breaking stories, and none from 10 PM to 7 AM. Weather watches wait for the morning too; warnings still come through. Chisme keeps your area (rounded to about 1 km) on its server so notifications match where you are; turning notifications off deletes it.

                    Motion

                    Turns off swipe animations, the pulsing location dot, radar autoplay and the food videos' autoplay.

                    Food videos

                    ¿Y la dieta?'s videos play by themselves as you scroll, muted. Turn this on to hear them (the 🔇 button in the feed does the same). On iPhone, Safari needs one tap in the feed before the first sound; after that, the next videos keep it.

                    Bigger the Pansa, Better the Chansa feed

                    ¿Y la dieta?'s Bigger the Pansa, Better the Chansa feed learns from what you watch, save and skip, and News remembers which stories you've seen or opened so it can show them in a different order. That's kept on this phone (Chisme only counts views anonymously, never who you are); this resets both.

                    Tía Chismosa

                    Your chats with la Tía and the interests she learns from what you tap are saved only on this phone (Forget me erases them). Each message you send goes to Chisme's server and to Google's Gemini AI, with the stories the app is showing, to get her answer. Chisme doesn't store it; Google may keep it under its own terms. Don't share private info.
