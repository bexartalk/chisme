      Event sources

      Prices, photos and times come straight from each listing. When a listing doesn't state a price we say “Check price” — always confirm on the event page. Weather outlooks are from the National Weather Service forecast for the venue's area (7 days out).

        Categories are tagged automatically from each listing's title and the source's own categories, so a few may land in the wrong bucket. “Free classes” only shows classes, talks and workshops the listing says are free.

        Food reviews come from public feeds: local creators' YouTube channels and local food coverage. Titles, photos and opinions are theirs.

          Weather refreshes every 10 minutes, news every 15 minutes, events every 30 minutes. Headlines link to the original publishers; story text is theirs, unedited. Times are shown in your local time. Location data: © OpenStreetMap contributors (Nominatim).
