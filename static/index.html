      Sports sources

      Scores, schedules, standings and news come from ESPN's public site API and the MLB Stats API. Your teams follow your location: the nearest NBA, NFL and MLB clubs, every team in your state first on the scoreboards, and the closest Minor League club within about 60 km (in San Antonio: the Spurs and the Double-A Missions). Story photos are the thumbnails ESPN publishes with each story and link to it; team photos are freely licensed Wikimedia Commons images, credited on each photo. No team logos. Headlines and stories belong to their publishers.

            Event sources

            Prices, photos and times come straight from each listing. When a listing doesn't state a price we say “Check price” — always confirm on the event page. Weather outlooks are from the National Weather Service forecast for the venue's area (7 days out).

              Categories are tagged automatically from each listing's title and the source's own categories, so a few may land in the wrong bucket. “Free classes” only shows classes, talks and workshops the listing says are free.

                  Alerts

                  Get a heads-up when there's new local chisme or an NWS warning near you. Nothing is sent until you turn it on.

                  At most one news alert every 45 minutes. Quiet hours are 10 PM – 7 AM: only weather warnings come through. Chisme keeps your area (rounded to about 1 km) on its server so alerts match where you are; turning alerts off deletes it.

                  Motion

                  Turns off swipe animations, the pulsing location dot and radar autoplay.

                  For You feed

                  ¿Cuál dieta?'s For You feed learns from what you watch, save and skip, and News remembers which stories you've seen or opened so it can show them in a different order. That's kept only on this phone and never sent anywhere; this resets both.

                  Tía Chismosa

                  Your chats with la Tía and the interests she learns from what you tap are kept only on this phone. Each message you send goes to Chisme's server with the stories the app is showing, is answered, and isn't stored.
