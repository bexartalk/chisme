Chisme Community Rules + Copyright Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Part 1: Community Rules

Órale, welcome to the pachanga! 🎉 Chisme is where San Antonio comes to spill the tea, share the memes, and keep up with the city. Keep it fun, keep it real, and don't be that primo who ruins the carne asada for everybody.

These rules are part of our Terms of Service. Break them and your post can get taken down, or your account can get the boot.

1. 18+ only, no exceptions

Chisme is for grown folks. If you're not 18 yet, come back later, mijo.

2. Kids are off-limits. Period.

Any content that sexualizes minors gets deleted, the account gets banned for good, and we report it to NCMEC and the cops. No warnings, no second chances.

3. Keep your clothes on

No nudity, no porn, no sexual content. And never post intimate pics of anybody without their consent. That's not chisme, that's a crime.

4. Chisme, not crueldad

Roast the Spurs' bench all you want. Don't harass, bully, stalk, or dogpile real people. No hate based on race, ethnicity, where somebody's from, religion, gender, sexuality, or disability.

5. No doxxing

Don't post anyone's address, phone number, workplace, license plate, private pics, or personal documents without their permission. Not even your ex's. Especially not your ex's.

6. No threats, no violence

No threatening anybody, no hyping violence, no promoting self-harm. If somebody's in danger, call 911. If you or someone you know is struggling, call or text 988.

7. Be you, not somebody else

No pretending to be another person, business, celebrity, or Chisme staff. Parody's cool if it's clearly labeled as parody.

8. Keep it legal

No selling drugs, guns, or hot merch. No scams, fraud, or schemes. If it's illegal in Texas or the U.S., it's illegal on Chisme.

9. Post your own stuff

Only upload photos, videos, and memes you made or have the right to share. Want to share a ChismeTV clip? Use the repost button. Don't rip and re-upload other people's content. (More in Part 2.)

10. No spam, no bots, no fakes

No fake accounts, fake likes, or bots. No flooding the comments. Got paid to post something? Say so with #ad or #sponsored. Chisme's own paid placements are always labeled "Sponsored."

11. Short and sweet

Videos max out at 60 seconds and display at 720p. Make it count. ⏱️

12. See something? Say something

Use Report for posts, comments, or profiles that break the rules. Use Block if somebody's bugging you. Don't file fake reports to get back at people.

What happens if you break the rules

Depending on how bad it is, we might remove the post, limit your account, suspend you, or ban you for good. Serious stuff (CSAM, real threats, doxxing) can get you banned on the first strike. Think we got it wrong? Email Arnulfoled@outlook.com and tell us why.

¡Ya estuvo! Now go have fun. 🌮

Reporting copyright infringement (takedown notice)

If you believe something on Chisme infringes your copyright, send our agent a written notice that includes all of the following:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature (typing your full legal name works for email), or the signature of someone authorized to act for the copyright owner.
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you say is being infringed (or, if there are several, a representative list).
  3. The specific content on Chisme you want removed, with enough detail for us to find it. Links to the exact posts or profiles are best.
  4. Your contact information: name, mailing address, phone number, and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use isn't authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

Incomplete notices may not be processed. When we receive a valid notice, we'll remove or disable access to the content and let the person who posted it know, including a copy of the notice (which may include your contact info).

Heads up: under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly makes false claims that material is infringing can be held liable for damages, including costs and attorneys' fees. Consider whether fair use or another exception applies before you send a notice. If you're not sure, talk to a lawyer.

Embedded clips: ChismeTV reposts play through YouTube or TikTok embeds, so the video files live on those platforms. We can remove the embed from Chisme, but to take the original video down you'll also need to contact YouTube or TikTok directly.

Counter-notice (if your content was removed)

If your content was removed and you believe it was a mistake or misidentification (for example, you own it, have a license, or it's fair use), you can send our agent a counter-notice that includes all of the following:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. A description of the content that was removed and where it appeared on Chisme before it was removed.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief the content was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your name, mailing address, and phone number.
  5. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for the judicial district where your address is located (or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which Chisme may be found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who sent the original notice or their agent.

When we receive a valid counter-notice, we'll send a copy to the person who filed the original notice. If they don't tell us within 10 business days that they've filed a lawsuit asking a court to keep the content down, we'll restore the content within 10 to 14 business days after we received your counter-notice.

Filing a false counter-notice can also make you liable for damages under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f).

Repeat infringer policy

Chisme will terminate the accounts of repeat infringers in appropriate circumstances. Here's how it works:

  • Each valid DMCA takedown against your account counts as a strike.
  • A strike is removed if a counter-notice results in your content being restored, or if the original notice is withdrawn.
  • If your account gets 3 strikes within 12 months, your account will be terminated.
  • We can terminate an account sooner, even on the first strike, for clear or intentional infringement (for example, an account set up mainly to re-upload other people's content).
  • Terminated users may not create new accounts.

Trademarks and other rights

For trademark, privacy, or impersonation complaints (which aren't covered by the DMCA), use the in-app Report button or email Arnulfoled@outlook.com.
